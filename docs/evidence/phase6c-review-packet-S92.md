## Red flags and safe actions

- RF-01 · Artificial deadline. The caller makes the practice decision sound time-limited. Take time to check the practice request.
- RF-02 · Claimed approval. A desk name, queue, or supervisor is offered as proof of identity. Use the fictional employee directory you already know.
- RF-03 · Isolation. The caller suggests keeping the training request away from colleagues. Consult the established training desk through your own channel.
- RF-07 · Threatened consequence. A delay, hold, or removal is presented as a consequence of pausing. Verify the stated consequence independently before deciding.
- RF-08 · Reward bait. A practice perk is linked to accepting the request on the call. Decline the offer until the familiar training channel confirms it.

- Pause before confirming any enrollment or benefit.
- Decline to provide private information or even a practice token to an unexpected caller.
